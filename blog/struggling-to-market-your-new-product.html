The Challenges of Launching New Products

Launching a new product is like running a hurdle race in a blindfold. Unseen obstacles are tripping you up at every turn, and you need to navigate them fast to win.

Let's explore these challenges for new product marketing teams:

Explaining the Unfamiliar

Imagine introducing a robot chef who dances while it cooks. Cool, right? But explaining how it makes life easier? Tough.

New products often have features never seen before, making it crucial to break down their benefits like bite-sized snacks. You need to answer the "what" (what it does) and "why" (how it solves problems) in a way anyone can understand.

Building Trust from Scratch

You're the new kid in town, and no one knows you. Building trust for a new brand is like climbing Mount Everest in flip-flops. You need to prove your product isn't just another fad, and explainer videos can be your trusty climbing gear.

Showcasing real-world benefits and happy customers helps you build trust faster than ever.

Standing Out in the Crowd

Think of established brands as giants in the playground. They have bigger toys and more friends. Standing out against them feels impossible.

Here, a product branding video can be your slingshot! These short, catchy stories highlight your unique features and show why your product is the coolest new toy everyone wants.

Finding Your Perfect Match

Not everyone needs a robot chef, right? Finding the right people interested in your product is like searching for gold coins on a beach. You need to understand their problems and desires to attract them.

Product demonstration videos targeted to specific groups can show how your product solves their unique challenges, making them feel like you found their buried treasure.

Making the Switch

People get comfy in their routines, so it's hard to convince them to switch. You need to show them, not just tell them, why your product is a life-changing upgrade.

With proper video advertising, you can showcase success stories, highlight time savings, and quantify the benefits of switching, giving them the push they need to ditch the burnt toast and embrace delicious breakfasts.

These are just the first hurdles new product launches face.

Remember, you're not alone! A well-crafted explainer video can be your secret weapon to overcome these challenges and launch your product like a champion, and if you collaborate with a professional and experienced animation studio, excelling in your business will be further easier for you.

Elements Of An Effective Product Explainer Video

To add some spice to your boring new product launch marketing campaign, you need a video bomb. And not just any video, but a compelling product explainer video. So, what makes these mini-masterpieces tick? Here's the recipe:

Great Openings

Start with a bang, not a whimper! Think of captivating visuals, intriguing questions, or even a touch of humor. This is your chance to grab attention and make viewers say, "Wait, what's this?"

Focus On Key Differentiators

Don't be shy about your special features! This is the time to highlight what makes you different from the rest of the crowd. Show off your unique selling points with pride, like a superhero revealing their superpowers.

Demonstrate Use Cases

Forget fancy jargon and focus on real-life problems your product solves. Demonstrate use cases that resonate with your target audience, showing how your product makes their lives easier, happier, or more productive.

Keep it Simple

Remember, not everyone speaks techie. Use clear visuals and relatable analogies to break down complex details into bite-sized pieces. Think of explaining rocket science to your grandma, but way cooler.

Drive To Take Action

Convince viewers to leap with a clear call to action. Tell them what you want them to do next, whether it's visiting your website, signing up for a free trial, or simply spreading the word. Make it easy for them to take the next step.

Include Client Testimonials

Testimonials are like gold stars for your product. Include client stories and happy faces to build trust and credibility. Hearing from satisfied customers is like getting a thumbs-up from friends, making your product even more appealing.

The result? A powerful explainer video that launches your product like a rocket into the success stratosphere!

Remember, an effective explainer video is more than just flashy visuals. It's about connecting with your audience, solving their problems, and inspiring them to take action. So, follow this recipe, sprinkle in some creativity, and watch your product take off!

Tips For Creating An Effective Explainer Video

If you are considering launching a new product, I highly recommend investing in an explainer video. A well-made explainer video can be a powerful tool that can help you overcome the challenges of launching a new product and achieving your marketing goals.

Here are some additional tips for creating an effective explainer video:

  • Keep your video short and sweet. Most people have short attention spans, so your video should be no longer than two minutes.
  • Use clear and concise language. Avoid jargon and technical terms that your audience may not understand.
  • Use visuals to support your message. A picture is worth a thousand words, so use visuals to help explain your product's features and benefits.
  • Make your video engaging. Use humor, storytelling, or other creative elements to keep your audience engaged.
  • Call to action. Tell your audience what you want them to do after watching your video. This could be to visit your website, sign up for your email list, or download your product.

Following these tips, you can create an explainer video that will help you launch your new product with success.

What is The Process of Making An Explainer Video?

Bringing an explainer video from concept to completion involves several key production stages:

Conceptualizing storyline ideas: Identify the narrative and flow that best conveys your messaging.

Scripting the video: Transform the concept into a compelling script incorporating voiceover and visuals.

Storyboarding: Map out the visual sequence scene-by-scene to align with the script.

Designing characters, graphics, and animation: Create customized visual assets, icons, and motion graphics tailored for your brand.

Voiceover talent and soundtrack: Add professional and engaging voiceover along with suitable background music.

Animating video frames: Use 2D or 3D techniques to bring the storyboards and script to life.

Adding final touches: Insert transitions, visual effects, and other post-production refinements.

Working with an experienced animation studio ensures a polished end product that captivates audiences. Take a look at the best practices for creating an animated explainer video. The entire explainer video production process typically takes 3-4 weeks.

How Much Does an Animated Explainer Video Cost?

When considering adding an explainer video to your marketing mix, one of the first questions is likely - what will be the budget needed for professional production?

The exact cost can vary substantially based on several key factors:

  • Video length - Longer runtimes require additional time for scripting, storyboarding, animation, and post-production. A 90-second video will cost more than a 60-second version.
  • Animation style - A 2D animated video can be produced more affordably compared to complex 3D animations and visual effects. 2D techniques provide great flexibility and style while keeping costs in check.
  • Graphics complexity - The more intricate the graphics, characters, environments, motion design elements, and overall visual sophistication, the more effort it takes to develop the assets. Simple tends to be more budget-friendly.
  • Choice of voiceover talent - Professional and captivating voice actors come at a premium compared to amateur voiceovers. Their talent fees can become substantial for longer videos.

As a general baseline, a 60-second 2D animated explainer video costs start from around $2000. This allows for crisp graphics, smooth animation, and engaging voiceover.

More advanced 3D animation videos with high-end visuals, intricate modeling, lifelike motion, and special effects will tend to cost upwards of $5000-$6000 or more. Top-tier animation studios that have the capabilities but also charge higher rates.

Working with an experienced explainer video production company gives you an accurate quote tailored to the length, style, and complexity needs of your specific video.

While not cheap, a well-produced animation video delivers outstanding ROI through greater customer engagement, conversions, and sales.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are animated videos more effective than live videos?

Ans: Animation allows you to demonstrate concepts and scenarios not feasible with live footage. Viewers also have fewer distractions focusing on characters vs. talking heads.

2. What makes for an effective explainer video script?

Ans: A tight script focused on key customer benefits, simple and digestible explanations, powerful analogies, and a clear CTA drives results.

Ans: Clean 2D animation and subtle motion allow software and tech to shine rather than overshadow the product demo.
