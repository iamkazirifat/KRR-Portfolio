About me

Writing that has to work, not just read well.

I've spent 9+ years writing content that has to work, not just read well. SEO content, technical breakdowns, B2B SaaS pages, video scripts. If a business needed someone to explain something complicated clearly, I was usually the one they called.

At CodeWatchers I wrote 900+ technical articles and helped drive 300% organic traffic growth for the site. At 10 Studio I wrote the B2B SaaS copy that helped close 12 enterprise partnerships. Right now I'm Senior Content Writer at WellsGroup, leading content and community strategy across three brands: WellsGroup, Transportvibe, and Safeeds.

I believe good writing is mostly editing, and that proof beats assertion every time. I don't write about things I can't verify myself. That's the standard I hold my portfolio to.
