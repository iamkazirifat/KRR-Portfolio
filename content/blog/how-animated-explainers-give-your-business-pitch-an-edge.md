Have you ever considered using an animated explainer video for service pitching to your client?

Video marketing is becoming an increasingly popular tactic to capture attention and demonstrate value to potential clients. But not just any video will do.

An animated explainer video specifically designed to highlight your service offerings can help create a memorable first impression and convey why prospects should choose you.

With compelling visuals and compact storytelling, service-focused explainers help simplify complex concepts and effectively showcase how you are different from others.

Combining vibrant motion graphics, engaging narrative arcs, and clear calls to action, your business can craft a video that entertains, educates, and excites viewers about working with you.

## Benefits of Using Explainer Videos to Showcase Services

An animated explainer video allows you to tell an engaging story that simplifies complex concepts. Well-crafted [motion graphics](https://10.studio/motion-graphics/) make your services memorable and convey a modern, cutting-edge brand image. Plus, animated video is highly shareable and reusable across platforms.

Let’s discuss what advantages you can avail yourself while using explainer video for service pitching -

1. Increased Memorability

Animation provides a highly engaging and entertaining medium that makes your explainer video more memorable.

Generally, videos are proven to boost brand recall by [more than 90%](https://www.insivia.com/2023-saas-marketing-report/#marketers:~:text=94%25%20of%20marketers%20say%20using%20video%20content%20has%20helped%20increase%20user%20understanding%20of%20a%20product%20or%20service.%20%28Wyzowl%29) compared to listening to words or reading texts. The vibrant visuals and motion graphics are simply more eye-catching and attention-grabbing.

Stylized characters, illustrated scenarios, and dynamic graphic elements can play unforgettable roles while demonstrating your services. The viewer/client is more likely to remember your unique animated promotion video than a generic corporate clip.

Animation also allows you to incorporate brand colors, fonts, logos, and other elements that reinforce your visual identity. This creates instant brand recognition and familiarity.

Since animation is flexible and imagination is the only limit, you can create metaphors and analogies to explain complex services in a sticky, and memorable fashion. Essentially, it taps into the brain's preference for visual learning.

Ultimately, [an animated commercial](https://10.studio/benefits-of-using-animated-commercials-for-small-businesses/) makes your brand more recognizable, distinctive, and unforgettable.

1. Enhanced Storytelling

Unlike a brochure or website copy, an animated explainer video allows you to tell an engaging story arc that draws in the viewer.

Animation provides creative license to set up an intriguing narrative that positions your hero character on a transformational journey. You can use relatable personas, humor, conflict, and resolution to showcase your services entertainingly.

This story-driven approach fosters emotional investment and keeps the prospect engaged throughout the entire video.

Well-executed narrative arcs in your animated promotion video also enable sophisticated messaging. Rather than a dry presentation of features, you can convey branded themes and core values through character-driven stories.

The prospect goes on a vicarious journey to understanding your mission. This helps humanize your business in their mind.

1. Simplified Concepts

A key advantage of explainer videos is the ability to simplify complex ideas and processes through dynamic motion graphics.

Animation provides the flexibility to illustrate how things work in a step-by-step fashion. Rather than explaining an intricate methodology in dense text, you can use illustrations and visual metaphors to walk the viewer through it in a digestible way.

This clarity is especially useful when showcasing technical or sophisticated services to prospects.

You can also effectively model cause-and-effect relationships. Demonstrating how your solutions positively impact clients is much more convincing through engaging animated scenarios.

Whether it's detailing a complicated workflow or showing how you address pain points, animated graphics allow you to untangle complexity. An animated promotion video will teach the viewer and build trust in your expertise.

1. Modern First Impression

In today's digital landscape, an animated explainer video on your website or social platforms conveys a modern, cutting-edge brand image.

Animation carries a sleek high-tech aesthetic that feels current. The motion design immediately signals that your company leverages the latest techniques and innovations to deliver services. This sets you apart from competitors still relying on static sales materials.

For services hoping to attract younger demographics, animation avoids feeling stodgy or dated. Overall, a polished animated video projects technology-forward thinking and contemporary appeal. Make a modern first impression that wins over today's mobile-first, visually-focused buyers.

## How To Craft a Captivating Story Arc

When creating an explainer video to showcase your services, storytelling is key. Here are some tips for crafting an engaging narrative:

- Focus on relatable user personas and scenarios. Viewers will connect more if the story centers on characters like them facing common problems. Build empathy by vividly illustrating their pains and needs using animation.
- Incorporate a transformative arc that shows how your services rescue users from their struggles. The animated promotion video should highlight the "before and after" - how you profoundly improve clients' lives. Use storytelling to create an emotional journey.
- Leverage humor and likable characters to make the video entertaining. Animation provides flexibility for humor that engages viewers. A bit of comedy makes your brand more approachable.
- End with summarizing the key takeaway in a memorable tagline or visual metaphor. This drives home the essential value you provide. Consider concluding with a clear call to action as well to convert viewers.
