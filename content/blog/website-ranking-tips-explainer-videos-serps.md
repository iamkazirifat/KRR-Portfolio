## Benefits of Using Videos for SEO

Adding [branded explainer videos](https://10.studio/why-branding-animation-is-a-must-for-modern-businesses/) to your website and content strategy provides multiple SEO benefits.

- Animated marketing videos allow you to showcase products and engagingly explain key features. This leads to higher click-through rates from search results.
- Videos keep visitors on your site longer, reducing bounce rates. Visitors are more likely to consume video content.
- Videos can rank for both generic and long-tail keyword searches related to your brand storytelling.
- Interactive, visual content builds trust and authority around your brand.

Videos enable you to connect with customers in a memorable and shareable format. Optimizing videos for SEO gives your brand more opportunities to rank highly and be discovered online.

## Tips to Optimize Explainer Video SEO

Now let’s discuss actionable best practices for optimizing on-page and off-page elements to maximize explainer video content for finding and engaging viewers.

### Focused Video Topic

Focus your explainer video on a specific, relevant topic that aligns with your overall content strategy. You can take a look at our guide on [types of explainer videos](https://10.studio/9-types-of-explainer-videos-for-business/) to boost your business.

For example, create an animated video that provides a step-by-step demo of your product's key features. Or tell a brand story that conveys your mission and values.

Keeping the topic targeted helps viewers quickly grasp the purpose and allows search engines to understand the video content.

### Keyword Integration

Integrate primary and secondary keywords naturally into your video title, description, and closed captions.

This boosts the chances of being discovered by searchers. Conduct keyword research to identify terms and phrases people use when searching for content related to your video topic.

Craft compelling, keyword-rich titles and meta descriptions that capture the topic.

### Compelling Video Title And Description

Add closed captions and interactive transcripts. Many viewers watch videos with the sound off. Enabling subtitles allows them to fully consume and interact with your content.

Transcripts also provide more opportunities to incorporate relevant keywords and phrases. This makes the video text-readable for search indexation.

### Closed Captions And Interactive Transcript

Implement schema markups like Video objects and FAQ pages to uphold rich search results and snippets.

Schema indicates to search engines the purpose and content of your video for better indexing. It can help your video thumbnail and description get displayed prominently in results.

### Contextual Backlinks

Build backlinks from related pages on your website and high-authority external sources. Contextual links signal relevance and authority to your niche video content.

Earn links through guest posts, mentions in roundup articles, and outreach to influencers/brands that could benefit from your explainer video topic.

### Schema Markup Implementation

Optimize SEO on the video landing page with meta titles/descriptions, headers, and copy that reinforce the video's focus keywords. This gives search engines more cues about the content.

## Measuring Video SEO Results

After optimizing and publishing your explainer video content, you'll want to track key metrics to determine the ROI and organic search success.

### Higher Rankings For Target Keywords

Monitor where your video lands for rankings on high-value keywords identified during the optimization process.

For example, if you focused on ranking for "product demo animation" and related long-tail variations, check if your animated product demo now shows up on the first page of Google results.

Higher rankings indicate your on-page and off-page optimization had a positive impact.

### Increased Organic Traffic

Keep an eye on traffic sources to see if views from organic searches grow over time. More visibility in search results should translate to greater amounts of website visitors from Google.

Traffic spikes can be revealed when Google indexes new video content or decides to rank it for additional keywords.

### Video View And Interaction Analytics

Dive into video-specific analytics within YouTube Creator Studio or your hosting platform. Look at total views, average percentage viewed, average view duration, likes, comments, shares, and click-throughs.

These metrics showcase how engaging your explainer video is for visitors. You can even see clicks on end cards and info cards added to your video to gauge interest.

### Lowered Overall Website Bounce Rate

See if your video integration lowered overall site bounce rates, meaning visitors who leave the landing page without further exploration.

Users who watch your full explanatory video tend to be more invested and inclined to click around your site or convert afterward. Lower bounce rates signal a positive user experience.

Regularly tracking video SEO metrics provides insight into content resonating with audiences and search algorithms.

Pay attention to trends and adjust optimization strategies as needed. ## Metrics To Determine ROI From Explainer Videos

Explainer videos require an investment of time and resources to produce. To determine the ROI of this marketing content, brands should track key metrics including -

- Rankings for target keywords and search terms related to your video SEO and optimization efforts.
- Traffic growth from organic search.
- Video view counts and engagement rates like percentage viewed, completion rates, and shares.
- Overall website bounce rates before and after video integration.
- Conversions on calls-to-action included in the video or on the landing page.

Seeing higher search rankings, increased organic traffic, strong video engagement signals, lower bounce rates, and more conversions provides quantitative data on the ROI of explainer videos.

Consider A/B testing video thumbnail images and titles to optimize click-through rates as well.

Analyzing metrics allows brands to refine their explainer video strategy and capitalize on formats and topics resonating most with audiences. Video analytics empower data-driven decisions for maximum ROI.
