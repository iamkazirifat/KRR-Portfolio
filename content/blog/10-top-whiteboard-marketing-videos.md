## What is a Whiteboard Animation Video?

Whiteboard animation refers to an engaging storytelling video style that utilizes simple hand-drawn visuals and diagrams to explain complex ideas, products, or services.

An artist illustrates the story on a white background via a stop-motion video as if actively drawing the images in front of an audience. This style is rapidly gaining popularity as an exceptional visual communication and brand marketing technique.

Whiteboard explainer videos allow businesses to showcase products, demonstrate how things work, promote thought leadership, and craft impactful brand stories in a simple, yet compelling way.

Some key advantages include -

- high engagement and view rates due to the entertaining, dynamic format;
- the ability to break down complicated details into easily understood concepts using custom illustrations specifically tailored for the brand;
- lower production costs compared to live video; and
- videos that keep the audience's attention throughout thanks to the evolving nature of “watching” a drawing unfold.

The unique, personal feel of whiteboard animation helps brands stand out and form an emotional connection. Simply put, these hand-drawn doodle videos foster engagement and brand recall like no other marketing content.
